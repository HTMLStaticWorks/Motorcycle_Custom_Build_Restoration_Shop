We are an independent motorcycle workshop dedicated to the art of mechanical restoration and custom fabrication.

01 // PHILOSOPHY

MECHANICAL
INTEGRITY FIRST.

"BUILT FOR THE ROAD, NOT THE PHOTOGRAPH."

At Ironvolt, we believe that a motorcycle must run as aggressively as it looks. The current custom scene is filled with machines built for photography rather than riding. We exist to build the opposite.

Every frame loop we weld, every wiring harness we construct, and every engine we rebuild is done with long-term reliability in mind. We strip away the unnecessary, refine what remains, and let the mechanical nature of the motorcycle speak for itself.

02 // CAPABILITIES

WORKSHOP TOOLS & PROCESSES

FABRICATION

In-house TIG and MIG welding. Lathe and mill machining for custom spacers, axles, and brackets. Sheet metal shaping using english wheel and shrinker/stretcher.

ENGINE BUILDING

Complete teardowns, cylinder honing, valve lapping, and precision measurement. We handle all four-stroke engine rebuilds in our dedicated clean room.

SURFACE FINISHING

Industrial vapor blasting cabinet for restoring aluminum engine cases to factory finish. Small-batch zinc plating for hardware restoration.
