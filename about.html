03 // CORE EXPERTISE

THE CRAFTSMANSHIP BEHIND THE MACHINE

Our workshop houses dedicated specialty zones, each equipped with precision industrial tools to execute every stage of your build under one roof.

01

POWERTRAIN & CLEAN-ROOM REBUILDING

Complete four-stroke engine tear-downs in our climate-controlled clean room. We perform ultrasonic case cleaning, industrial vapor-blasting, three-angle valve seat cutting, dynamic crankshaft balancing, and electronic ignition retrofits.

  • Vapor-honing cases to factory-spec satin aluminum finish
  • Piston-to-cylinder clearance honed within ±0.01mm
  • Multi-carburetor rebuilds with custom jetting for ethanol-free & pump gas
02

CHASSIS & TIG METAL FABRICATION

Chassis modifications on our precision laser alignment jig. We fabricate custom subframes, monoshock linkages, inverted fork conversions, and detab vintage frames without compromising structural rigidity.

  • 4130 Aircraft-grade chromoly tubing & custom gusseting
  • Purged sanitary TIG welding on titanium and stainless steel
  • Rake and trail geometry calculations for predictable handling
03

SHEET METAL COACHBUILDING

Traditional metal shaping using English wheels, power hammers, and shot bags. We hand-form bespoke aluminum fuel tanks, cafe racer tail cowls, side panels, and aerodynamic fairings from flat alloy sheet.

  • 3003-H14 & 5052 hand-formed aluminum gas tanks
  • Internal baffling, petcock bungs, and pressure testing to 5 PSI
  • Flawless brushed alloy, mirror-polished, or concours multi-stage paint
04

ELECTRICAL & CAN-BUS MODERNIZATION

Eliminating fragile 40-year-old wiring harnesses with military-grade Raychem DR-25 heat shrink and German Motogadget digital M-Unit controllers. Clean, invisible routing hidden within bars and chassis tubes.

  • Keyless RFID proximity start and smartphone telemetry
  • High-output stator & MOSFET regulator/rectifier upgrades
  • Ultra-compact lithium-ion battery integration
