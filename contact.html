ANSWERS.

Common questions about visiting the workshop and starting a project.

Start An Enquiry
Do I need to bring my bike in person?

Yes — we do an in-person assessment for every project before committing to a scope. Photos are a great starting point but we need to see the machine in the flesh before quoting.

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 48 hours. For urgent matters, call us directly during workshop hours.

Are you open to walk-ins?

The workshop operates by appointment only. This ensures each client gets full attention from the team when they visit. Book via the enquiry form or phone.

Can I visit during the build?

Absolutely. We schedule milestone check-ins at key stages so you can see progress in person. Regular photo updates are also sent throughout.

Do you ship bikes after completion?

We can arrange enclosed transport for completed builds anywhere in the continental US. International shipping can be organized on request with a specialist freight partner.
