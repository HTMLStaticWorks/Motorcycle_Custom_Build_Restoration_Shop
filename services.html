01

RESTORATION

Returning classic and vintage motorcycles to factory condition or better. We approach restoration with respect for the machine's history, combining modern precision with period-correct aesthetics.

  • Strip-down assessment and frame inspection
  • Sourcing and rebuilding original components
  • Vapor blasting and component refinishing
  • Period-correct paint matching and hardware
  • Modern internal upgrades for reliability
View Process Details
02

FABRICATION

When off-the-shelf parts compromise the vision, we build it. From custom exhausts to complete subframe modifications, our fabrication process ensures every piece fits the aesthetic and mechanical intent of the build.

  • TIG & MIG welding for steel, aluminum, and titanium
  • Custom exhaust headers and muffler systems
  • Frame loop modifications and structural bracing
  • Hand-shaped sheet metal work (tanks, cowls, fenders)
  • Custom brackets, battery boxes, and mounting solutions
03

ENGINE REBUILDS

Reliability starts at the core. We perform comprehensive engine work from top-end refreshes to complete overhauls, ensuring the motor runs flawlessly without claiming unrealistic performance figures.

  • Complete engine teardown, cleaning, and assessment
  • Vapor blasting cases and cylinder heads
  • Bearing, seal, and gasket replacement
  • Cylinder honing and piston ring replacement
  • Carburetor rebuilding, ultrasonic cleaning, and tuning
