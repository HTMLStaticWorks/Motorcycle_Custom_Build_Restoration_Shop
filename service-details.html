Enquire About Restoration
01 // THE SCOPE

WHAT THE PROCESS INCLUDES

A true restoration goes beyond cosmetics. We tear the motorcycle down to the bare frame, evaluating every single nut, bolt, and bearing. The goal is a machine that looks museum-quality but runs reliably for the modern road.

Complete motorcycle teardown, cataloging, and structural inspection.
Frame repair, sandblasting, and period-correct powder coating.
Complete engine rebuild including vapor blasting cases and cylinder honing.
Sourcing NOS (New Old Stock) parts or high-quality reproductions.
Carburetor ultrasonic cleaning, rebuilding, and precise tuning.
Suspension rebuild and wheel lacing with polished hubs.
Original OEM color-matched paintwork and hand-applied pinstriping.
Zinc plating hardware and re-chroming original exhaust systems.
02 // WORKSHOP

THE PROCESS

1. ASSESSMENT

Evaluating condition and sourcing rare parts.

2. DISASSEMBLY

Careful teardown to bare frame and cataloging.

3. RESTORATION

Engine rebuild, vapor blasting, and metal prep.

4. REBUILD

Reassembly using NOS parts and rebuilt components.

5. FINISH

Tuning, break-in miles, and final detailing.
