03 // FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

Do you supply the base motorcycle?

We can source a donor bike for you as part of the project budget, or you can supply your own. Sourcing it ourselves often guarantees we start with a platform well-suited for the build direction.

How long does a full custom build take?

Depending on scope, a full build generally takes between 4 to 8 months. This accounts for teardown, fabrication, specialist parts or finishes (like chrome plating), and final tuning.

Do you offer payment plans?

Builds use milestone payments. Typically: a deposit to schedule and source parts, a mid-build payment after fabrication, and the final balance upon completion and sign-off.
